# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft — for prototype phase. Not a substitute for legal advice.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Family health profiles | Symptom triage, family health management | Per user; deletable on request |
| Symptom data, triage results | Triage functionality | Per session; historical data retained for trend analysis |
| Medication records | Medication management | Per user; deletable on request |
| Health documents | Records vault | Per user; deletable on request |
| LLM prompts/responses | Triage functionality (server-side only) | Minimized; not persisted by default |

## Data Storage

- Family health data is stored in the application's database (prototype: local development only).
- No data is shared with third parties except as required for LLM inference (Gemini API, server-side only).
- Health data is highly sensitive and handled with extra care under UAE PDPL and DHA data handling requirements.

## Your Rights

Under UAE PDPL and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to health record retention requirements)
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)

## Important Notice

Tabibi is a prototype and is not medical advice. Triage results should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional medical evaluation.

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the maintainer.
